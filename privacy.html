Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Rope Challenge ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Rope Challenge does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name or email address directly.

The app uses third-party services that may collect certain data, including advertising identifiers and usage data, to provide advertising and process purchases:

  • Google AdMob — serves advertisements and may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier / IDFA), usage data, and diagnostics for ad delivery and measurement.
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases (the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase) and may process purchase-related data.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the app will ask for your permission before tracking your activity for advertising purposes, in accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You can decline, and you can change this anytime in your device settings.

How Data Is Used

Collected data is used to display ads, measure ad performance, process purchases, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal data.

Data Storage

Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
